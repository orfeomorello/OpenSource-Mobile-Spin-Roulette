MobileSpinRoulette

MobileSpinRoulette is a free, open-source roulette game for phones, tablets and desktop. It uses virtual points only. There is no real-money gambling, no purchases, no cash-out and no prizes.

The table

WHAT YOU CAN DO

  • European table (single 0, 37 pockets) and American table (0 and 00, 38 pockets)
  • Inside and outside bets, plus European racetrack calls
  • Magnetic snap on the felt for straight, split, corner, street, six-line and more
  • Rebet, Double and Undo
  • Strategy creator: save up to 24 layouts and place a full hand in one tap
  • Session statistics and score trend
  • Music, sound effects and wheel animation you can turn off
  • Nine languages: English, Italian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Korean, Japanese and Chinese
  • Installable Progressive Web App; play from the device cache after the first load

HOW TO PLAY

Choose a chip, tap the felt, press Spin. A new profile starts with 2,000,000 virtual points. If the score reaches zero, starting a new game restores that amount. Score, settings and strategies stay on this device. There is no account and no analytics.
