Mobile Roulette – No cash is a free, open-source roulette game for phones and tablets. It uses virtual points only. There is no real-money gambling, no advertising, no analytics, no purchases, no cash-out, and no prizes.

Features:
• European table with 37 pockets
• American table with 38 pockets
• Inside and outside bets
• European racetrack calls
• Rebet, Double, and Undo
• Up to 24 saved betting strategies
• Session statistics and score trend
• Nine interface languages
• Local offline play with no account

Choose a chip, tap the felt, and press Spin. Score, settings, and strategies remain on your device.

The software is licensed under GNU GPL v3 or later. The F-Droid edition excludes the optional non-free music playlist; gameplay and synthesized sound effects remain available.
